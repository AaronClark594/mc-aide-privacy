The MC Aide

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains how The MC Aide handles information when you use its Discord bot and connected website.

Information we collect

We collect information needed to operate the service, such as Discord user IDs, usernames, server and role information, account details, information you submit, and records created through the features you use. We may also collect technical information, such as IP addresses and diagnostic logs.

How we use information

We use information to provide the service, manage access, support community activities, maintain records, and resolve technical issues. Discord member information is used to identify staff under selected roles for staff coordination and attendance.

Access and sharing

Information is available to service operators and authorized users as needed for the service. Information displayed through community features may be visible to members with access to those features or channels. Discord handles information sent through its platform under its Privacy Policy.

Storage and retention

Records are retained for community operations and history until manually removed. Leaving the server does not automatically delete your information. Copies may remain in backups or Discord messages.

Cookies

The connected website uses cookies for authentication and browser storage for preferences. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes visitor information under GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Contact and requests

For privacy questions or to request access, correction, or deletion of your information, open a support ticket in the Discord server where you use The MC Aide. Staff may verify your identity before handling your request.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
